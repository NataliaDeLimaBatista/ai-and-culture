## Documentaries

- *Coded Bias* (2020) — Examines bias in facial recognition, beginning with research at MIT.
- *AlphaGo* (2017) — Follows DeepMind’s system as it competes against top Go players.
- *The Social Dilemma* (2020) — Explores how recommendation algorithms shape behavior and attention.
- *Do You Trust This Computer?* (2018) — Looks at how AI is used in infrastructure, defense, finance, and media.
- *Hi, A.I.* (2019) — Follows people interacting with social robots in everyday settings.
- *Smartest Machine on Earth: Can a Computer Win Jeopardy!?* (2011) — Follows the development of IBM Watson and its match against human champions.
- *The Artificial Intelligence Revolution* (2024, title translated from Portuguese) — Surveys the social impact of generative AI.
- *Artificial Democracies: Elections in the Age of AI* (2024, title translated from Portuguese) — Looks at how algorithms influence campaigns, elections, and public opinion.

## Series

- *Black Mirror* — Selected episodes: “Be Right Back,” “White Christmas,” “Hang the DJ,” and “Joan Is Awful.”
- *Westworld* (2016–2022) — Android hosts in a theme park begin to show signs of consciousness.
- *Person of Interest* (2011–2016) — A surveillance system predicts violent events, and competing groups seek control of it.
- *Devs* (2020) — A secretive company develops a system that can simulate the past and future.
- *Humans* (2015–2018) — Some android workers known as synths begin to develop consciousness.
- *Cassandra* (2025) — A family moves into a home governed by a virtual assistant named Cassandra.
- *The Age of A.I.* (2019) — A documentary series about applications of AI across different fields.
- *The Future Of* (2022) — A documentary series about how technology could change everyday life.

## Animes

- *Ghost in the Shell* (1995) — A special operations unit investigates cybercrime in a connected society.
- *Psycho-Pass* (2012–2019, TV seasons) — The Sibyl System assesses people’s likelihood of committing crimes.
- *Serial Experiments Lain* (1998) — A student becomes involved with a network that blurs the boundary between virtual and physical life.
- *Neon Genesis Evangelion* (1995–1996) — Pilots in a mysterious war depend on support systems such as the MAGI supercomputers.
- *Time of Eve* (2008–2009) — A café treats humans and androids as equals, raising questions about how they relate to one another.
