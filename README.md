# AI and Culture

A watchlist and reading collection on AI in film, art, museums, cultural heritage, and public engagement.

## Start here

Pair *Coded Bias* with *Ex Machina* to compare how a documentary and a fictional film approach power and accountability. For a museum case study, start with the Nasher Museum’s curatorial chatbot.

## What’s inside

- [Watchlist](watchlist.md) — Films, documentaries, series, and animes
- [Culture and heritage](culture-and-heritage.md) — Museum projects, art, heritage research, and cultural policy
- [Public engagement checklist](public-engagement-checklist.md) — Questions for outreach, science communication, and cultural projects
